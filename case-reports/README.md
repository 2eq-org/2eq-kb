# Case Reports (Anecdotal)

## Prominent disclaimer
**All material on this page is anecdotal.**  
Case reports do **not** constitute evidence for the Singular Motivational Subversion Hypothesis (SMSH).

They are included solely to document subjective phenomenology and to generate hypotheses that may be tested independently.

---

## Purpose of this page
This page collects reports in which individuals describe psychological shifts that appear *consistent* with the absence or reduction of the hypothesized motivational subversion.

These reports:
- may be incomplete,
- may be mistaken,
- may be culturally mediated,
- and may admit of alternative explanations.

Their inclusion does not imply endorsement or validation.

---

## Inclusion criteria for reports
A report may be included here only if it:

1. Describes a **claimed durable change** (not a transient mood or insight).
2. Includes **context** (life circumstances, stressors).
3. Addresses **duration** (how long the change persisted).
4. Mentions **stress tests** (what challenged the change).
5. Acknowledges **alternative explanations**.
6. Avoids claims of universality or superiority.

Reports failing these criteria should not be included.

---

## Standard report format
All reports should follow this structure:

### Report ID
A neutral identifier (no names unless explicit consent is given).

### Context
Relevant background information (age range, circumstances, psychological history if known).

### Claimed shift
What changed, in the reporter’s own words.

### Duration
How long the change has persisted.

### Stress tests
What pressures or challenges were encountered afterward.

### Observed differences
Specific differences in motivation, affect, cognition, or behavior.

### Alternative explanations considered
Possible interpretations other than removal of a stabilizing dynamic.

### Current status
Ongoing, reverted, unclear, or unknown.

---

## Interpretation boundaries
- Case reports **do not** validate the hypothesis.
- Similar reports exist in many domains (religious conversion, psychedelic experience, meditative insight, affective episodes).
- No inference about prevalence or reproducibility should be drawn.

The relevance of reports depends entirely on whether they suggest **discriminating predictions** that can be tested.

---

## Ethical cautions
- No report should be used to create identity hierarchies.
- No report should be framed as a model to emulate.
- No individual should be encouraged to pursue psychological risk based on these accounts.

---

## Status
This section is **open** and intentionally conservative.

Reports may be added, revised, or removed as understanding improves.
